my version of "working hard"

Technically I'm “working hard”, long hours learning lots of complex content, busy sleepless days organising and running events, going outside and talking to people. But I have this sneaking suspicion that I haven't been working hard, and I've been making myself so busy I haven't noticed. I'm trying to think back to when I felt like I worked hard, I haven't worked hard in years … I don't think I've ever worked hard actually. That sounds so absurd! Didn't I define what working hard is?
